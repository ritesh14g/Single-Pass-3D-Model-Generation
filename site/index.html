One drone pass in.
A georeferenced 3D model out.

Reconstructs terrain, structures and vegetation from a single drone video and its position log. Classical structure-from-motion supplies the measured geometry; a feed-forward depth network fills thinly seen surface, but only where it agrees with measurement. The model is placed on the map from the drone's own GPS with no ground control points, and every surface is labelled measured, inferred or never seen. Six formats come out, each re-opened to verify it.

The problem

A single pass sees every surface from only a few angles.

Systems built on that either leave holes, or invent geometry without saying so.

This one measures what was seen, infers only where the inference is checked against measurement, and outlines the rest as gaps.

What it does

Eight capabilities, in the order the video meets them

00 · INPUT CHECK

Refuses bad input in seconds

Reads the container, the position log and the video↔GPS agreement before any compute is spent, and says how to fix what it finds.

01 · INGEST

Keeps the frames that matter

Selects frames to hit a target overlap instead of sampling at a fixed interval, and gates out frames too blurred to reconstruct from.

overlap-targeted selectionforward-backward LKNCC verification
02 · CONDITIONING

Cleans each frame and the GPS track

Suppresses compression blocking, evens out exposure, masks moving objects, and filters position outliers before anything is matched.

03 · RECONSTRUCTION

GPS-anchored SfM and dense surface

Solves every camera with structure-from-motion, refines against the GPS track, then builds a dense cloud, a mesh and a texture.

03 · TRACK B

Learned depth, only where it agrees

A feed-forward depth network adds detail where stereo is thin, scaled to the measured surface and refused wherever it disagrees.

04 · OCCLUSION HANDLING

Every surface labelled by how well it was seen

Zone 1 is measured, Zone 2 is thin or anchored, Zone 3 was never observed and is outlined as a gap, never filled.

On the map from the drone's own GPS

Fits the model to the flight track, converts to a projected coordinate system and resolves what the altitudes were measured from.

Prepared flights

Input check

Before reconstructing anything, the system reads the file itself, the position log and the agreement between the two. Each check reports a measured value, what it means, and how to fix it when it is worth fixing. A blocking result stops the run here rather than after twenty minutes of computation.

Ingest

Decodes the video and keeps the frames that overlap each other enough to reconstruct from, rejecting blurred ones.

Conditioning

Cleans each kept frame: compression blocking, exposure drift, shadows, moving objects, and noisy GPS.

Reconstruction

Solves where the camera was for every frame, then builds the dense surface and paints the photographs onto it.

Occlusion handling

Labels every surface by how well it was really seen, fills thin areas from anchored depth, and outlines what was never seen at all.

Georeferencing

Places the model on the map from the GPS track and resolves what the heights are measured from.
