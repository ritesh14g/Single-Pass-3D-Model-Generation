Procedural illustration · nodes → surface → map

One drone pass in.
A georeferenced 3D model out.

Reconstructs terrain, structures and vegetation from a single drone video and its position log. Classical structure-from-motion supplies the measured geometry; a feed-forward depth network fills thinly seen surface, but only where it agrees with measurement. The model is placed on the map from the drone's own GPS with no ground control points, and every surface is labelled measured, inferred or never seen. Six formats come out, each re-opened to verify it.
