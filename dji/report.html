Limitations (stated first)

  • Speed: 44.2 min for 1.7 min of video projects to 256 min for a 10-minute video against the §9 target of 15 min (device cuda; S4-8).
  • Camera centres agree with GPS to 2.136 m RMS: absolute position is limited by the telemetry; relative distances within Zone 1 are more reliable.
  • 12.0% of the ground the cameras saw was never observed (Zone 3): flagged in gaps.geojson and the viewer, never filled, excluded from accuracy.
  • No independent reference surface was given for this run: accuracy is agreement with the telemetry, not with ground truth (pass --reference with a lidar DSM or LAS).
  • The degradation benchmark was not run for this report (python -m src.cli bench degrade).
  • The single-pass simulation needs a multi-strip survey; not run for this report.
